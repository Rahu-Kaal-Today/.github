### What is Rahu Kaal?

According to ancient texts, **Rahu** is a malefic celestial entity. The period ruled by Rahu is thought to amplify negative energies, making it unsuitable for critical activities. Many people avoid signing contracts, purchasing assets, or starting new ventures during this window. However, Rahu Kaal is considered ideal for introspective tasks, like planning or resolving conflicts, as it encourages caution.

### How to Calculate Rahu Kaal

Rahu Kaal is calculated based on the division of daytime (sunrise to sunset) into eight equal parts. Each part is assigned to a specific planetary ruler, with the 8th segment governed by Rahu. The duration of Rahu Kaal changes daily due to seasonal shifts in sunrise and sunset timings. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

1. **Determine Sunrise and Sunset Times**  
   Check local timings for your location.  
   *Example:*  
   - Sunrise: 6:00 AM  
   - Sunset: 6:00 PM  

2. **Calculate Day Duration**  
   Subtract sunrise time from sunset time.  
   *Example:*  
   `6:00 PM – 6:00 AM = 12 hours`.

3. **Divide Daytime into 8 Equal Parts**  
   Divide the total duration by 8.  
   *Example:*  
   `12 hours ÷ 8 = 1.5 hours (90 minutes per segment)`.

4. **Identify Rahu Kaal Based on the Day**  
   Each weekday corresponds to a specific segment:

   | Day       | Rahu Kaal Segment |
   |-----------|-------------------|
   | Monday    | 2nd Part          |
   | Tuesday   | 7th Part          |
   | Wednesday | 5th Part          |
   | Thursday  | 6th Part          |
   | Friday    | 4th Part          |
   | Saturday  | 3rd Part          |
   | Sunday    | 8th Part          |

   *Example:* On Monday, Rahu Kaal falls during the 2nd segment (7:30 AM – 9:00 AM in a 12-hour day).
